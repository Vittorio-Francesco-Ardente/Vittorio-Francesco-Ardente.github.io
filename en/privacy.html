Privacy

This is a static personal website: it has no forms, no analytics, no cookies, and it loads nothing from external services. Fonts, libraries and images are hosted with the site. Here is what happens to your data when you visit it.

Data controller

Vittorio Francesco Ardente, Dalmine (BG), Italy, vittorio.ardente.07@gmail.com. No data protection officer has been appointed, as it is not required.

What data and why

Providing data is optional: without technical data, however, the site cannot be displayed, and without an email address I cannot reply. I do no profiling, I do not sell or share data, and I make no automated decisions.

  • Technical browsing data. As with any website, the hosting service receives your IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and operating system. They are used only to show you the site and protect it from abuse (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f GDPR). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes this data as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Emails you send me. The site has no forms: the contact buttons open your email program. If you write to me, I use your address and message only to reply (pre-contractual measures or legitimate interest, art. 6.1.b and 6.1.f). I keep messages for as long as the exchange requires and for at most 24 months, unless it leads to a working relationship. Messages go through my email provider.
  • External links (LinkedIn, GitHub, sun-fai.org). They are plain links, with no widgets: no data is sent to those services until you click. After the click, their own privacy notices apply.

Transfers outside the European Union

GitHub is a US company: technical browsing data may be processed in the United States. GitHub states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (European Commission adequacy decision of 10 July 2023) and uses the standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing (arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to vittorio.ardente.07@gmail.com: I reply within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Cookies and local storage

The site uses no cookies. It only uses browser storage for two technical functions, which stay on your device and are never sent to me or to third parties:

NameTypePurposeDuration
vfa-temalocalStorageremembers the theme you chose (season or holiday)until you clear it in your browser
vfa-localelocalStorageremembers the language you choseuntil you clear it in your browser

They are technical tools needed for a service you request (art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code; Garante cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021): that is why no consent and no banner are needed. You can delete them at any time from your browser settings.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, collects no user-entered data and loads no third-party scripts. If I ever add a contact form or visit statistics, this page will be updated before they go live, stating the provider, the data collected and how long it is kept.
